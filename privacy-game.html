Privacy Policy — ExplApp Mobile Game

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy describes privacy practices that may apply to an ExplApp mobile game. Features and third-party services depend on the particular game release. We will update this page with game-specific details where necessary.

1. Game data

Gameplay progress, scores and settings may be processed or stored locally on the device to support the game's functions. Some multiplayer or online features, if enabled, may require network communication and limited technical data. We do not claim that every ExplApp game operates exclusively offline.

2. Advertising and analytics

If the game includes third-party advertising or analytics software, those providers may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, app interactions, crash reports and other diagnostic data as permitted by applicable law and user choices. Such processing is governed by their own privacy policies. Please consult the in-game notices to identify enabled providers. Information may be used to display and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve reliability.

3. Permissions

The game may request internet access and other device permissions only when a feature needs them. Your device's settings provide controls for permissions and advertising privacy choices.

4. Sharing of information

ExplApp does not directly sell gameplay information. Relevant technical data may be processed by integrated providers to operate advertising, diagnostics, multiplayer features or other services disclosed in the game. Users should check those providers' privacy documentation for further details.

5. Children

We seek to respect applicable children's privacy requirements. A game's intended audience and its use of advertising or external services must match its Google Play age targeting, applicable consent rules and actual software configuration. Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy concerns.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally saved game data may be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the game, though files intentionally exported elsewhere may remain. Third-party providers independently determine their retention periods under their policies. You may contact us about deletion of personal data controlled by ExplApp.

7. Security

We aim to use proportionate safeguards for data under our control. However, no technical system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information and may have rights to object to or restrict certain processing. Please send requests to the contact address below.

9. Updates

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in the game, its third-party services or legal requirements. Updates will be reflected in the effective date.
